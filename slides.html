✓ IDEMPOTENT REPAIR ARCHITECTURE
01
Deterministic Hashing & Upsert

Records are keyed by normalized DOI. Re-running the pipeline multiple times never creates duplicate vectors or state explosion in ChromaDB.

02
Atomic Quarantine Partitioning

Corrupted records that fail GX checks are isolated into dead-letter storage, protecting downstream vector embedding workloads.

03
Automated Recovery Workflow

When corruption is detected, the pipeline automatically fetches verified raw snapshots, sanitizes features, and re-indexes the vector space.
